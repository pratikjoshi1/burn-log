      2. Add Find Health Samples: Type is Steps, Start Date is Today.

      4. Add Round Number, then Copy to Clipboard.

      How calories are estimated

      Calories are estimates: MET × body weight (kg) × hours, with MET values from the Compendium of Physical Activities; cardio MET is picked from your pace when you give a distance. Lifting uses MET 3.5 (light), 5.0 (moderate) or 6.0 (hard) for the time from your first set to saving. Estimated 1-rep max uses the Epley formula. Real numbers vary with fitness and effort.

      Used for calories. This unit also sets distance (kg → km, lb → mi). Lifting weights have their own kg/lb switch on the Lift tab.
